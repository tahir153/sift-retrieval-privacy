SIFT Retrieval Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026 · Publisher: Tahir Habib

In short: SIFT Retrieval works entirely on your computer. It does not collect, send, sell or share any personal data. Your documents and your search index never leave your device.

What SIFT Retrieval does

SIFT Retrieval is a Windows app that indexes documents in folders you choose and lets you search them by meaning. All reading, indexing and searching happens locally on your device. The app does not need an internet connection and does not connect to any server.

Information the app stores on your device

To work, the app keeps the following on your computer only:

  • A search index built from the text of the documents in the folders you add
  • The list of folders you chose to watch
  • Your recent searches and the files you opened from the app, shown on the Statistics page
  • Your settings, such as language and theme, and technical log files used for troubleshooting

This information is stored in the app's data folder on your device. It is never uploaded or shared.

Information we collect

None. SIFT Retrieval has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no telemetry and no tracking. The publisher receives no information about you, your documents or how you use the app.

Microsoft Store

If you install SIFT Retrieval from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may process information about your purchase and installation under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. That information is handled by Microsoft, not by the publisher of this app.

Your control

  • Clear search history, opened files and indexing history at any time on the Statistics page.
  • Remove a folder or individual files from the index in the app.
  • Delete everything the app stored by using Settings > Reset application, or by uninstalling the app.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the publisher, Tahir Habib, through the support contact listed on the SIFT Retrieval page in the Microsoft Store.
